# Audio sources

The game uses the following CC0 recordings and music.

- Orchard ambience uses the bird and river recordings from [Park ambiences](https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences) by Thimras. The tracks were mixed, filtered and crossfaded into a continuous loop.
- Background music is [Forget Me Not](https://opengameart.org/content/forget-me-not) by Kistol. The game uses the looped piano version, transcoded to MP3 and mixed at low volume.
- Harvest sounds are trimmed from [Rustling leaves](https://freesound.org/people/Elandre01/sounds/594400/) by Elandre01.
- Menu and basket sounds are trimmed and pitched from [Wooden Tap](https://freesound.org/people/AidanSounds/sounds/704935/) by AidanSounds.
- Watering audio is trimmed from [Water Pouring Quickly Into Glass](https://freesound.org/people/jbeetle/sounds/274777/) by jbeetle.
- The coin sound is trimmed from [Coin Splash](https://opengameart.org/content/coin-splash) by LordTomorrow.
- The short speech cue is edited from [Single Bird Chirp](https://freesound.org/people/alegemaate/sounds/233255/) by Allan Legemaate.

The game chime was made for Hedgehog Garden.
