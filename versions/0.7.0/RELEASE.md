# Hedgehog Garden 0.7.0

## Research and power

The scientist visits the oldest stall once production is unlocked and the garden has 10,000 coins. Accept the commission yourself. Hedgehogs and tractors can deliver the 14 requested goods to its separate account. The final payment of 25,000 coins opens Research in «Хозяйство».

Solve a manual challenge for your first research points, then choose Electricity or Irrigation. Coal and workshop fuel run the first generator. Later projects add biofuel, batteries, waterwheels, turbines, automatic watering, and electric factory conversions. Machines on the same grid slow down together during a shortage.

## Hedgehogs and the estate

- Preview a replacement recruit for apples. Each reroll for the same vacancy raises the next cost.
- Train individual stats up to 20, discover inherited traits at the family station, and choose from three wardrobe previews.
- Earn rare job-specific Unique perks. Each hedgehog can keep up to three perks.
- Save the roster’s workplaces, jobs, and favorites as a preset. The retirement lodge leaves 15 minutes to retrieve a hedgehog if there is room.
- Explore 16 new general upgrades, new visitors and product dialogue, leisure stations, and a revised «Хозяйство» screen with a map, power view, and stock filters.
- Export or reset progress from the main menu. The river and forest are quieter, and music is slightly louder.

## Saves and installation

Existing 0.6.0 browser and Android saves load in 0.7.0, including separate inventories and shipments already in progress. Existing crew presets keep the workers they covered. Export a JSON backup from the game before changing builds. On Android, install the signed APK over the installed app to retain local data.
