# Changelog

## 0.7.2.1

- Added a hidden, animated picnic scene on the map at half its original size.
- Added a guided opening for gardens without a hedgehog, with highlighted targets, basket and apple counters, and a reveal at the first unlock.
- Картошка’s tutorial messages now dim the game until acknowledged.
- Hedgehogs have more solo, friendly, and work-related lines. Their shared chat cooldown is 31 seconds.

See the [0.7.2.1 release notes](versions/0.7.2.1/RELEASE.md).

## 0.7.2

- Recruit previews and hedgehog profiles suggest jobs based on the worker’s stats and perks and the workplaces available in the current garden.
- Extended perks to market work, freight, tractor operation, workshops and factories, mining, power, research, and training. Factories can have an optional hedgehog assigned to improve batches.

See the [0.7.2 release notes](versions/0.7.2/RELEASE.md).

## 0.7.1.1

- Removed undiscovered feature names from the bulk job assignment preview.
- Fixed the global upgrade tree viewport and keyboard focus when zoomed in.
- Android checks for new releases while the game is open, retries temporary failures, and offers a manual check in Settings.
- Removed the main-menu animation button. The background respects the device’s reduced-motion setting.

See the [0.7.1.1 release notes](versions/0.7.1.1/RELEASE.md).

## 0.7.1

- Redesigned the main menu with an animated orchard and an original pixel-art logo for «Ёжиковый сад». Background motion can be paused and follows the device’s reduced-motion setting.
- Added a browser favicon and mobile home-screen icons. The Android launcher icon adapts to the device’s icon shape.

See the [0.7.1 release notes](versions/0.7.1/RELEASE.md).

## 0.7.0

- The scientist’s 14-good commission and final 25,000-coin payment unlock research. Start with Electricity or Irrigation and earn research points from puzzles or a powered lab.
- Added local power grids, three generator fuels, batteries, renewable generation, automated irrigation, and factory retrofits.
- Added paid recruit rerolls, training, inheritance, wardrobe previews, retirement, job-specific Unique perks, and full workforce presets.
- Added 16 general upgrades, 12 visitors including the scientist, product-specific lines, decorations, and minigames.
- Reworked «Хозяйство» with a live estate map, power overview, and warehouse filters. Added main-menu reset and export, removed «Рейс» from basket badges, and adjusted the music and ambience mix.

See the [0.7.0 release notes](versions/0.7.0/RELEASE.md).

## 0.6.0

- Houses have separate inventories. The household screen shows each location and the total across houses. Existing shared stock migrates into the original house.
- Cargo moves between locations. Dedicated porters can supply workshops and clear their outputs. Tractors pick up fuel from homes, follow connected roads with short approaches, and can serve two nearby stops with one load. Destinations can have different freight priorities or be paused.
- Mines extract raw coal. Workshops turn it into fuel. Workshops, factories, and mines have individual upgrades. Workshop speed, batch size, and storage upgrades are required before factory conversion.
- Added a first-production walkthrough and provisions made from apples and berries. Added six upgrade-tree branches, including bonuses specific to the first orchard and first farm.
- Recruiting costs rise by 16% per hire. Added recruit previews, selection and assignment of multiple hedgehogs, saved crews, and 30 rollable perks across six rarities.
- Fences have visible gates used by worker routing. Built roads have pixel texture, and stations show working animations. Added customer dialogue and a quiet looped piano track.

See the [0.6.0 release notes](versions/0.6.0/RELEASE.md) for save and update details.

## 0.5.2.2

- Made the first hedgehog goal visible, restored manual basket delivery after hiring, and improved placement and assignment feedback.
- Added recent sales and a wild berry shortcut. Fixed several production interruptions and reduced growth in late improvement-point rewards.

See the [0.5.2.2 release notes](versions/0.5.2.2/RELEASE.md).
